How to Activate Fubo TV

  1. Download the Fubo TV app on your streaming device.
  2. Open the app and select Sign In.
  3. Copy the activation code displayed on your TV.
  4. Visit the activation website on your phone or computer.
  5. Redeem Fubo TV activation code and sign into your account.
  6. Start watching live sports, movies, and entertainment.
